description: |-
  Alongside the gatherings, the project opens onto a cultural-historical inquiry into artist-run spaces across cultures, with particular attention to the Soviet Union and Los Angeles in the 1960s and 1970s. Read against these histories, the apartment becomes a way to ask how a home, a building, and a space held in common can begin to overlap.

  KV(ART)IRNIK turns an apartment into a provisional home for more than ten artists, their exhibitions, music, performances, discussions, spiritual practices, catalogs, and art books. The project asks how to turn a fake home into a space for everyone, and how to make a space for everyone feel like home.
