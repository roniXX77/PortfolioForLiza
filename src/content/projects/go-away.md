  Acrylic on canvas, 2026

  “Ket” means “go away” in Kazakh. The blindfolded lynx in my painting takes its cue from one on a fifteenth-century medal of Leonello d’Este. Said to see through walls, the animal appears on another medal beside the motto “What you see, do not see.” I imagine its golden eyes covered, but its sense of what is coming still intact – intuition, premonition, perhaps the impulse to leave.
