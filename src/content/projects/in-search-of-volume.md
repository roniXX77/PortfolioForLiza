description: |-
  In Search of Volume was a participatory, learner-centered laboratory for Garage MCA’s teen audience. Across six weeks of four-hour meetings, we explored autofiction, the museum’s spaces through Visual Thinking Strategies (VTS), and performance practices. I developed the program’s concept, methodology, and structure, as well as constructivist tools for learning together. With museum technicians and performance educators, I co-developed a final work of experimental choreography and autofiction with the participants, who wrote and performed it.

  Our work moved through the fluidity between bodies and performance, bringing contemporary political irony into conversation with the kitsch of late Soviet and 1990s culture. The museum’s digital archives and physical spaces became part of the laboratory, as did the institution itself during a silent anti-war protest.
