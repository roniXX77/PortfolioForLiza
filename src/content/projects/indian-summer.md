Digital video, 19 seconds

Shot in Moscow during the summer of 2024, this 19-second performance follows my entry into a river, an image I felt I had to set down before leaving. The gesture becomes a private act of cleansing and a way to think alongside Boris Ryzhy about youth, the end of summer, and the impossibility of entering the same water twice.

*To surrender to the current of cold September water – and come back to shore.*
