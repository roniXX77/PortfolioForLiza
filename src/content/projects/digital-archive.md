description: |-
  Personal Public is an interactive digital archive built from my writing, sound recordings and moving images. The visitor enters through footage of the city, reaches a staged “502 bad gateway” and chooses one of five figures. Each opens a different way of reading the material: the Author’s desktop folders, the Agent in Love’s email inbox, Alex the Diver’s film, the Rain’s audio playlist or the Dead Tourist’s departures board.

  These interfaces make personal material look searchable, playable and filed away, though no route through it offers a complete account of a life. A poem appears as a document in a folder, a letter as an email, a memory as a flight detail. The change of format alters the distance between writer and reader, letting autobiography move between confession and character. The visitor decides what to open, what to leave unread and when to stop. I am interested in what happens to an intimate archive once its order belongs to someone else.
