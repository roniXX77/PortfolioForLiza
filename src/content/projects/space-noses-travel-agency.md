description: |-
  The question “Do I Look Like a Tourist?” travels through Space Noses Travel Agency as both a performance and a set of business cards. Photographic documentation is available, with video documentation upon request.

  The project moves through the city of Moscow’s urban absurdism and the pressure of censorship, asking what kinds of characters can become visible there and what they must disguise. I build the project’s characters around the tourist: a figure who can feel at home in a city, foreign to it, or both at once. Tourism and immigration blur into one another in the feeling of being a stranger, and in the wish to find a home.
